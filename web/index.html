The pipeline

One invoice, four agents, one auditable decision.

Each agent has one job and its own tools. Code owns the math and every amount. Grok extracts, reasons and critiques.

  1. 1

    Ingest

    Reads PDF, TXT, CSV, JSON and XML. Fixes OCR slips like "2O26". Grok repairs anything the parser cannot read, with schema checks fed back up to 3 times.

    ↺ Self-corrects on schema errors
  3. 2

    Validate + risk

    Checks every line against the SQLite inventory, the math, dates, FX, prices, vendors and duplicates. Scores fraud signals from 0 to 100.

    35 flag codes, each with evidence
  5. 3

    Approve

    Blocking flags reject at once. A VP agent judges the rest. Over $10K, at or above $9,500, or risk 50+, a critic challenges the VP for up to 2 rounds. Still split? A human decides.

    VP proposes⇄Critic pushes back
  7. 4

    Pay or reject

    Approved invoices call the mock bank once, guarded by an idempotency key and retries. Rejections are logged with reasons. Holds go to a review queue.
