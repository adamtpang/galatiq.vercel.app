The problem

Acme pays people to retype invoices. It costs them.

Invoices arrive by email in every format. Staff copy the data by hand, check it against a shaky inventory database, chase a VP over email, then pay.

The pipeline

One invoice, four agents, one auditable decision.

Each agent has one job and its own tools. Deterministic code owns the math. Grok extracts, reasons and critiques.

  1. 1

    Ingest

    Reads PDF, TXT, CSV, JSON and XML. Grok extracts vendor, items, quantities, total and due date into a strict schema.

    ↺ Self-corrects when the schema or math fails
  3. 2

    Validate

    Checks every line against the SQLite inventory. Flags stock overruns, unknown items, bad dates, negative quantities and totals that do not add up.

    Tools: query_inventory, check_math
  5. 3

    Approve

    A VP agent proposes a decision. A critic agent challenges it. They revise until they agree or escalate. Anything over $10K gets extra scrutiny.

    VP proposes⇄Critic pushes back
  7. 4

    Pay or reject

    Approved invoices call the mock payment function once. Rejections are logged with the reasons, so a human can see exactly why.

    ApprovedRejectedNeeds review
